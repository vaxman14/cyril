The problem

The industry runs on bodies it never repairs

Kitchens are physical arenas. The work is athletic — the recovery culture is nonexistent.

Feet, knees & back

A career on concrete floors. Standing 12+ hours destroys joints faster than most contact sports.

Sleep after service

Off the line at midnight, wired on adrenaline until 3 AM, back at it by 10. Recovery never happens.

Dehydration on the line

Hours next to open flame in 100°F+ heat. Most chefs finish service down liters — and refill with caffeine.

Burnout as a badge

The culture treats exhaustion as proof of commitment. The result: careers that end a decade early.
